About Us

FriendsFlow is a modern professional networking platform designed to connect individuals, share industry insights, and foster meaningful discussions.

Built with security and user experience in mind, our platform features real-time data synchronization, client-side AI content moderation to ensure a safe environment, and a clean, responsive interface.

Whether you are an independent developer, digital marketer, or tech enthusiast, FriendsFlow provides a seamless and secure space to build your professional network.

Privacy Policy & Terms

1. Information We Collect

When you use our platform, we collect authentication data via Google (Name, Email Address, and Profile Picture) to create your account. We also store your User-Generated Content (posts, links, comments) in our cloud database to display in the network feed. We do not use tracking cookies; we only use local storage for your UI preferences.

2. How We Use Your Information

Your name, profile picture, and headline are displayed publicly to other registered users. We use your data solely to provide and maintain the platform. We do not sell, rent, or share your personal data with third-party advertisers.

3. AI Image Moderation

To maintain a safe environment, we utilize a client-side AI safety model. Images are scanned directly inside your browser. Inappropriate content is blocked locally and is never transmitted to our servers.

4. Acceptable Use

By posting, you agree to post no spam, fraudulent offers, or malicious links. Explicit or violent content is strictly prohibited. Users must interact professionally.

5. Admin Rights & Third-Party

Administrators reserve the right to remove any post or account violating these terms. Our platform relies on Google Firebase and GitHub Pages; by using our app, you acknowledge their respective privacy policies.
